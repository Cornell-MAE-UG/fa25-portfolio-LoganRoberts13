Problem Set 3 helped me better understand how conduction, convection, and radiation work together in real heat transfer problems. The photovoltaic panel problem(1) showed me how to build a thermal resistance network and connect temperature changes to system performance, since silicon temperature affects electrical efficiency. The rod problem(2) helped me see how fin behavior and exposed length can be used to control surface temperature in a practical design. The square pin fin problem(3) also reinforced how geometry, material properties, and convection conditions affect fin efficiency and thermal resistance. I learned that heat transfer is not just about solving equations, but about modeling a physical system using reasonable assumptions. The assignment made it clearer how thermal analysis can guide engineering decisions about dimensions, materials, and operating limits.
